隱私權政策

編織計算器不蒐集你的資料，也不會追蹤你。

繁體中文

編織計算器不蒐集、傳送、出售或分享個人資料。不需要帳號、不含廣告、不含分析 SDK，也不會跨 App 或網站追蹤你。

輸入內容、儲存草稿、單位偏好與 App 內評分狀態只保留在你裝置的 UserDefaults，並不會上傳。你可在「設定」移除記住的輸入內容；刪除 App 會移除該裝置上的本機狀態，但仍受裝置備份與還原設定影響。

所有計算、說明、複製文字與分享文字皆可離線使用。App Store、隱私頁與電子郵件連結會讓你離開 App，之後適用該目的地的隱私權做法。

English

Knitting Calculator does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It requires no account, has no advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Inputs, saved drafts, unit preference, and app-local rating state stay in UserDefaults on your device and are not uploaded. You can remove remembered inputs in Settings; deleting the app removes its local state from that device, subject to device backup and restore settings.

Calculations, help, copied text, and generated share text work offline. App Store, privacy, and email links leave the app and are governed by the destination’s privacy practices.
