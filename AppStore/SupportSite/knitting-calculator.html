開始使用

  • 密度：輸入量測樣本的長度與針數或排數，再輸入目標長度。查看密度、精確針數與最接近整數的建議。
  • 平均加減針：選擇單一排或多排，輸入起始與目標針數。結果會先顯示摘要，可再展開棒針或鉤針步驟。
  • 草稿與單位：最近輸入內容及單位偏好只會儲存在你的裝置的 UserDefaults。可在「設定」移除記住的輸入內容；刪除 App 會移除該裝置上的本機狀態。

計算、說明、複製與產生的分享文字皆可離線使用。選擇 App Store、隱私頁或電子郵件連結時會離開 App。

Getting started

  • Gauge: Enter a measured sample length and stitch or row count, then the target length. Review gauge, exact count, and the nearest-whole-stitch recommendation.
  • Even increases and decreases: Choose one row or multiple rows, then enter start and target stitch counts. The result starts with a summary; expand it for knitting or crochet steps.
  • Drafts and units: Recent inputs and unit preference stay in UserDefaults on your device. Remove remembered inputs in Settings; deleting the app removes its local state from that device.

Calculations, help, copied text, and generated share text work offline. Choosing an App Store, privacy, or email link leaves the app.
