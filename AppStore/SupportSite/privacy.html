隱私權政策
Privacy Policy

KnitNote 的工作資料留在你的裝置上。不需要帳號；作品資料儲存在裝置上。下方說明購買與廣告服務的資料處理。

繁體中文

KnitNote 是一款在 iPhone、iPad、Apple Watch 與 Mac 上使用的編織工具。如有問題，請寄信至 lzz.1999@icloud.com。

我們不收集的資料

KnitNote 不需要帳號，不會把作品、照片、織圖、毛線資料、筆記、日記或備份傳送到開發者營運的伺服器。

裝置上的資料

作品、計數器、織圖閱讀狀態、筆記、手寫標記、日記、毛線庫與設定儲存在你的裝置上。Apple Watch 透過 Apple 的裝置間通訊與配對 iPhone 同步作品和計數器；開發者不會收到內容。

相機、照片與檔案

只有當你主動拍照時才會要求相機權限。照片、PDF、圖片與備份套件只會在你使用系統選擇器挑選後，為你要求的匯入或還原操作而讀取。

備份與還原

只有當你主動匯出時才會建立外部備份。你選擇儲存位置並自行管理；還原只讀取你明確選擇的 KnitNote 備份套件。

購買與 App Store

購買、付款與商店交易由 Apple 處理。KnitNote 不會收到付款卡號或 Apple 帳號密碼；Apple 處理的資料受其政策規範。

廣告與隱私選擇

從加入廣告的版本起，未購買永久解鎖的使用者可能在 iPhone／iPad 的作品首頁看到 Google AdMob 橫幅廣告。已確認永久解鎖或符合早期付費使用者資格者不顯示廣告。Mac 與 Apple Watch 不顯示廣告。

Google 廣告服務可能處理 IP 位址（可推估大致位置）、裝置識別碼、App 資訊、廣告互動與診斷資料，用於提供廣告、防止詐欺、分析及衡量服務運作。KnitNote 不會將作品、照片、織圖、毛線資料、筆記或日記內容加入廣告請求。App 關閉發佈商第一方 ID 與發佈商隱私個人化設定；這不代表廣告 SDK 完全不處理資料。

適用時，App 會顯示 Google 的隱私選擇訊息，你可以透過設定中的「廣告隱私選項」調整選擇。廣告無法載入時，App 仍依原有試用與購買權限運作。你也可以從設定主動回報不適當廣告。Google 處理的資料及其保存方式，請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。這些廣告條款不適用於未包含廣告的舊版 KnitNote。

保存、刪除與兒童隱私

資料保留在裝置上，直到你在 App 內刪除、移除 App 或清除裝置資料。外部備份由你自行刪除。上述裝置資料的保存方式不涵蓋 Google 廣告服務處理的資料。

政策變更

更新時會在本頁標示新的生效日期；重大變更會隨 App 更新或支援頁面說明。

English

KnitNote is a knitting tool for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac. Questions may be sent to lzz.1999@icloud.com.

Data we do not collect

KnitNote requires no account and does not transmit projects, photos, patterns, yarn details, notes, journals, or backups to a developer-operated server.

Data on your devices

Projects, counters, pattern-reading state, notes, handwritten marks, journals, yarn inventory, and settings are stored on your device. Apple Watch synchronizes project and counter state with its paired iPhone through Apple's device-to-device communication. The developer does not receive this content.

Camera, photos, and files

KnitNote requests camera access only when you choose to take a photo. Photos, PDFs, images, and backup packages are read only after you select them with a system picker and only for the import or restore operation you requested.

Backup and restore

An external backup package is created only when you explicitly export one. You choose its storage location and manage it. Restore reads only the KnitNote backup package you explicitly select.

Purchase and the App Store

Apple processes app purchases, payments, and store transactions. KnitNote does not receive your payment-card number or Apple Account password. Apple's own policies govern data Apple processes.

Advertising and privacy choices

Starting with the version that introduces advertising, people without lifetime access may see a Google AdMob banner on the iPhone/iPad projects home screen. Verified lifetime purchasers and eligible legacy paid owners do not see ads. Mac and Apple Watch do not display ads.

Google advertising services may process IP addresses (which can estimate general location), device identifiers, app information, advertising interactions and diagnostic data to deliver ads, prevent fraud, perform analytics and measure service operation. KnitNote does not add projects, photos, patterns, yarn details, notes or journals to ad requests. The app disables publisher first-party ID and publisher privacy personalization; this does not mean the advertising SDK processes no data.

Where applicable, the app presents Google's privacy choices. You can revisit choices through Advertising privacy options in Settings and initiate a report about an inappropriate ad. If ads cannot load, the app continues to follow its existing trial and purchase permissions. For Google's handling and retention of data, see Google's Privacy Policy. These advertising provisions do not apply to older versions of KnitNote that contain no ads.

Retention, deletion, and children's privacy

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app, remove the app, or erase device data. You delete exported backups from their chosen location. This retention description covers local project data, not data processed by Google advertising services.

Policy changes

The updated effective date will appear here. Material changes will be described with an app update or on the support site.
