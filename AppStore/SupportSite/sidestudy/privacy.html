隱私說明

你選擇匯入的教材會複製到 App 的儲存空間。筆記、題目、答案與來源頁碼也在裝置上保存。這個版本不需要 App 帳號，未加入廣告、分析服務或開發者自建雲端同步。

你主動使用「檔案」選取內容時，可能選用 iCloud Drive 或其他供應商。系統備份及診斷也可能處理裝置資料。這些服務有各自的設定與政策；本說明不代表資料絕不離開裝置。

保存與移除

請在離開前保存修改；未保存草稿不保證在 App 結束後保留。刪除 App 可能移除其本機資料；系統備份中的副本依系統服務的設定處理。更新時請勿刪除 App。

聯絡

支援信箱為 lzz.1999@icloud.com。若你主動寄送問題，信件內容會經過電子郵件服務處理；請避免附上不必要的私人教材、筆記或其他敏感資料。

Privacy

Local app data

Materials you choose to import are copied into app storage. Notes, questions, answers and source-page links are also saved on your device. This version has no app account, advertising, analytics service or developer-operated cloud sync.

Files providers and system services

When you choose content in Files, you may use iCloud Drive or another provider. System backups and diagnostics may also process device data. These services have their own settings and policies; this notice does not claim that data can never leave your device.

Retention and removal

Save changes before leaving. Unsaved drafts are not guaranteed to survive app termination. Deleting the app may remove its local data. Copies in system backups are managed under the system service settings. Do not delete the app when updating.

Contact

Contact lzz.1999@icloud.com for support. If you choose to email a question, your message is processed through email services. Avoid sending unnecessary private study materials, notes or other sensitive information.
