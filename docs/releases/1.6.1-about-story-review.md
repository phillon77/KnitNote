## 繁體中文（zh-Hant）

### 開發故事

有人問我，App 開頭的那幅畫是誰畫的。

那是女兒送給太太的生日禮物。畫裡正在織毛線的人是我太太，旁邊熟睡的兔子，是女兒的寵物 Lemon。

女兒上大學後，Lemon 就留在家裡，由我們夫妻倆照顧。牠陪伴了我們很多年，直到 2025 年離開。現在每次看到這幅畫，除了想起女兒的心意，也會想起 Lemon 曾經陪伴我們的那些日子。

就像畫裡一樣，我太太非常喜歡織毛線。她試用過許多相關的 App，最後留下來使用的那一款，免費版只能使用基本功能，想用進階功能就必須持續訂閱。

於是她問我，能不能寫一個真正符合她需要的 App：功能實用、操作簡單，而且不用一直訂閱，只要付費一次，就可以長久使用。

就這樣，這個 App 誕生了。

### 建議使用方式

執行 KnitNote，我建議大家使用 iPad，或 Mac 等螢幕較大的電腦。看織圖或教學影片時會比較清楚，眼睛也不容易疲勞；使用計數器時，也比較不會不小心按錯。

## English（en）

### The Story Behind KnitNote

People sometimes ask me who painted the picture shown when the app opens.

It was a birthday gift from our daughter to my wife. The woman knitting in the painting is my wife, and the rabbit sleeping beside her is our daughter's pet, Lemon.

When our daughter went to university, Lemon stayed at home, and my wife and I took care of our little rabbit. Lemon was with us for many years, until we had to say goodbye in 2025. Whenever I see this painting now, I remember both our daughter's thoughtful gift and the days we shared with Lemon.

Just as in the painting, my wife loves knitting. She tried many knitting apps. The one she eventually settled on offered only basic features for free; the more advanced features required an ongoing subscription.

So she asked me whether I could make an app that really suited her needs: practical features, simple controls, and a single payment instead of a continuing subscription, so she could keep using it for years.

And that is how this app came to be.

### A Suggestion for Using KnitNote

When using KnitNote, I recommend an iPad or a computer with a larger screen, such as a Mac. Patterns and tutorial videos are easier to see, viewing feels more comfortable, and you are less likely to tap the wrong counter control by accident.
